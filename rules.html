Premise

Queen Carmilla's Castle has appeared at the edge of your village. The Queen is rumored to be afflicted with vampirism, and whispers spread that she has sent her servants into the village under cover of darkness.

By evening, the village's priest is found dead.

The Mayor (DM) holds absolute authority over the village, serving as its sole judge, jailer, and keeper of order. With suspicion falling on everyone, the villagers must uncover the vampire hiding among them before the town runs out of nights.

There are only three consecutive nights to discover the truth. When the third night ends, so does the game.

Goal

  • Play time: 1 hr. Max
  • Villagers — Rid the village of the vampirism disease. All Vampires must be found; staked or caged until their second death (by the sun).
  • Vampires — Grow in numbers, feed on the Villagers, and survive until the third night when Carmilla's army arrives.

Phases

  • Introductions (6 minutes) — Only at game start. Players state their name and role. (May include subterfuge)
  • Encounter (6 minutes) — Players roll a 1d6 in turn until time runs out. Order continues the next night.
  • Discussion (5 minutes) — Players are allowed to make accusations, share notes and clues, and convince others during this time only.
  • Voting (3 minutes) — Votes casted cannot be changed. Timer can stop early if all votes are in. Someone will end up in the cage. If the Vampire is selected, they will burn once the sun comes up. Anyone else becomes an a la carte option for the vampire.
  • Sleep (3 minutes) — The only time special abilities can be played, as well as saving throws. Players must DM their actions during.

Player Setup

  1. Have your Kindroid Scenario created and share the Discord screen with the kin.
  2. Have the Dice Page open in a new tab.
  3. Open the Chat menu and ensure captions are enabled for kin to keep up to date with the conversation.

Saving Throws

Villager Attacked Once By Vampire → DM Rolls Life/Death.

  • Wings → Safe, but you incur Vampire Bite Marks.
  • Skull → Ghost Mode. Create a 150 char max message from beyond the grave to The Seer and roll a d20.
    • 1–5: No message.
    • 6–10: The Seer receives a symbolic vision pointing toward the Villager's suspicion.
    • 11–20: The message crosses the veil exactly as written.

Familiar Death

Roll a d20.

  • 1–12: Fail. Tell them the villagers succeeded. Invite them to spectate and post their kin's complaints and lamentations.
  • 13–20: Pass. Give them the Vampire card. They may elect a victim for the following night.
